=== PHISHING INDICATOR ANALYSIS ===

1. SENDER DOMAIN
From:
helpdesk@corp-itsupport-helpdesk.example

Observation:
The sender domain differs from the apparent corporate domain:
corp.example

Assessment:
Potential sender-domain impersonation.

2. REPLY-TO MISMATCH
Reply-To:
helpdesk.reply@webmail-inbox.example

Observation:
The Reply-To address uses a different domain from the sender address
and the apparent corporate domain.

Assessment:
Potential attempt to redirect replies to a different mailbox.

3. EMAIL AUTHENTICATION
SPF: softfail
DKIM: none
DMARC: fail

Assessment:
The message did not successfully authenticate as the claimed corporate
sender.

4. URGENCY
Subject:
Action Required: Your password expires in 24 hours

Observation:
The email creates urgency by warning that the user's mailbox may be
suspended.

Assessment:
Urgency and account-lockout pressure are phishing indicators.

5. HYPERLINK MISMATCH
Visible text:
https://sso.corp.example/password-reset

Actual destination:
http://corp-itsupport-helpdesk.example/reset?uid=employee

Observation:
The visible URL does not match the actual hyperlink destination.

Assessment:
Potential attempt to make the destination appear more trustworthy.

6. PASSWORD RESET THEME
Observation:
The email requests immediate account verification through a password-reset
link.

Assessment:
The credential-reset theme is consistent with phishing behavior.

7. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
The synthetic email contains multiple phishing indicators, including
sender-domain inconsistency, Reply-To mismatch, failed email authentication,
urgency, and a visible-link/actual-destination mismatch.

Risk Assessment:
HIGH within the context of this synthetic phishing-analysis exercise.

Note:
This is a controlled training sample. No conclusion about real-world
malicious infrastructure should be made from this sample.
